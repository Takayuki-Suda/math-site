📐 Area of a Triangle

Move the picture and get a feel for why it's "base × height ÷ 2".

Area = base × height ÷ 2

Look at it step by step

Summary

①
When you surround the triangle exactly with a rectangle,
a red and a blue triangle appear outside it.
②
When you fold over the red and blue,
they fit exactly on top of the green triangle.
→ red + blue = green (the same size!)
③
Inside the rectangle there is green plus red-and-blue, exactly two of them.
So green (the triangle) = rectangle ÷ 2
= base × height ÷ 2!
For any triangle base × height ÷ 2
