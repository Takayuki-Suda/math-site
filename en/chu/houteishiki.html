What Is an Equation?

STAGE 2 ── The guardian Balance Chimera. Break its balance by "doing the same thing to both sides" and reveal the mystery number x.

Think with a balance scale
Steps to solve it
x = 3
Check: 2×3 = 6 ✓
Why do we use x?
"I added 3 to a number and got 7. What was the number?"

In your head, you can tell "7 minus 3 is 4". But when problems get complicated, your head alone isn't enough.

That's where the method of "calling the unknown number x" comes in.

Write x + 3 = 7, subtract 3 from both sides, and you get x = 4.
This rule of "doing the same thing to both sides" is the heart of equations.

When a balance scale is level, it stays level even if you take the same weight off both sides. It's exactly the same idea.
