The heart of Fourier analysis

Fourier series
Write a periodic function as an infinite sum of sine waves. The coefficients come from inner products (integrals).
Square wave
(4/π)Σ sin((2k−1)x)/(2k−1). Only odd-order sine waves appear.
Fourier transform
F(ω)=∫f(t)e^(−iωt)dt. From time to frequency. The inverse transform brings it back.
Uncertainty principle
Δt·Δω ≥ 1/2. Time resolution and frequency resolution trade off against each other.
