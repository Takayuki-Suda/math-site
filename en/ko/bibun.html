3Rules for differentiating

Definition of the derivative

f'(x) = limh→0 [f(x+h)−f(x)] / h

The "average rate of change" between two points on a curve,
taken to the limit as h → 0, is the derivative.
That's the "slope at that instant" = the slope of the tangent line.
