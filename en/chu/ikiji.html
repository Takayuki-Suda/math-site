🐺 Wilderness of Straight Lines
y = ax + b

STAGE 3 ── The Graph Wolf runs along a straight line. Move a and b and read its every move.

y = 1x + 0
a slope (the steepness of the graph) 1
b intercept (where it crosses the y-axis) 0
A linear function is a relationship where each time x goes up by 1, y goes up (or down) by a fixed amount.

It is written as y = ax + b, and its graph is always a straight line.

For example, if you walk 80 m every minute, the distance y after x minutes is y = 80x (a=80, b=0).
a (slope) tells you "how much y increases when x increases by 1".

•a is large (positive) → the graph is a steep uphill
•a is small (positive) → the graph is a gentle uphill
•a is negative → the graph goes downhill
•a = 0 → a horizontal line (y = b)

Move a with the slider and check!
b (intercept) tells you "what y is when x = 0", in other words where the line crosses the y-axis.

•b = 3 → the graph passes through (0, 3)
•b = -2 → the graph passes through (0, -2)
•b = 0 → the graph passes through the origin (0, 0)

Changing b doesn't change the slope (the line just shifts). Check it with the slider!
Here's how to read a and b from a graph.

① Look at the point where the graph crosses the y-axis → that's b
② Move 1 to the right and count how much y increases → that's a
