🦂 Ruins of the Right Angle ― Seeing the Pythagorean Theorem Through "Area"

STAGE 5 ── The final guardian, the Pythagoras Scorpion. Attach squares to the three sides: green + orange has exactly the same area as blue. Only those who see through this secret open the road to the boss, "The Wall of Functions".

a² + b² = c²

Move the sliders

Proof (count the area in two ways)

Setup Arrange four right triangles with sides a, b, and c (the hypotenuse) inside a large square with side (a + b).
① Area of the large square Its side is (a + b), so
　area of the large square = (a + b)²　= a² + 2ab + b²
② Write an equation for the area large square = inner square (c²) + 4 right triangles
　a² + 2ab + b²　=　c²　+　4 × (½ab)
　a² + 2ab + b²　=　c²　+　2ab
③ Subtract 2ab from both sides a² + b² = c²　　　　　…proof complete ✓
a² + b² = c²　　✓
